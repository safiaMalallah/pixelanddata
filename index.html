What is DATA & PIXEL?

DATA & PIXEL is a child-centered educational world that introduces data science concepts through Dataying™, a playful approach that helps children observe, ask questions, collect clues, find patterns, and share discoveries.

Why It Matters

Children are surrounded by technology every day. DATA & PIXEL encourages them to become creators, thinkers, and problem solvers rather than only consumers of technology.

Detective Theme

Students learn by investigating problems, looking for clues, comparing information, and sharing what they discover in age-appropriate ways.

Interactive Experiences

The DATA & PIXEL world includes cartoon animations, mascot-led sessions, hands-on activities, songs, badges, stickers, learning events, and teacher lessons.

Meet the Characters

Data and Pixel are two curious robot characters who guide children through data science detective adventures.

DATA

DATA is curious, energetic, and always ready to look for clues.

PIXEL

PIXEL helps students organize ideas, find patterns, and explain discoveries.
